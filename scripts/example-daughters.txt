# I Raised My Daughters Alone For 15 Years

## a man sitting alone on a couch late at night, one warm lamp, quiet empty living room, melancholy
Fifteen years. That is how long I gave everything I had. No partner. No help. And I never once asked to be thanked.

## a tired working father in a diner kitchen at night, worn uniform, warm practical light
I worked two jobs. Sometimes three. I packed lunches at five in the morning. I signed permission slips in parking lots. I was the only father who also showed up as the mother.

## a father laughing with his two teenage daughters in a sunlit kitchen, golden hour, candid warm family moment
I watched them grow. Maya became fierce and funny. Zoe became quiet and brilliant. Every night they said the same five words. Goodnight Dad, we love you.

## a thanksgiving dinner table, a young woman standing to raise a wine glass, warm candlelight, family around the table
Last Thanksgiving, my daughters stood to give a toast. Maya raised her glass and smiled at the man beside her. Thank you for being the dad we always needed. Neither one of them turned my way.

## a man alone facing a bathroom mirror at night, dim light, emotional restraint, moody low key lighting
I did not cry at the table. I excused myself. In that cold bathroom I made a choice. To be proud of them anyway. Even if it broke me.
